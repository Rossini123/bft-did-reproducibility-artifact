# Reproducibility scope
Offline verification of recorded outcomes and published numerical displays. E1/E3 quorum, membership-index, boundary, receipt/state and rejection predicates are recalculated by a clean reader; cryptographic ECDSA recovery from signature bytes is not repeated. Relative signer indices are derived from recorded recovered-signers/membership lists and do not prove signature authenticity independently. E4 verifies reconstructed metrics and selected display series, not raw trace acceptance/control predicate replay. No experiment rerun capability, full implementation release, universal reliability, or public availability is claimed. Historical Sepolia is normal-path evidence from locally generated watcher wallets. Submission-call-to-receipt observations are not Ethereum finality. Analytical cost scenarios are hypothetical, not measurements.
